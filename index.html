A real Net Promoter Score — the proposal

Score every simulated patient 0–10 from their own experience, classify with the standard bands, and take promoters minus detractors — the actual NPS definition, using per-patient delay data the simulation already records. Full reasoning: NPS-PROPOSAL.md.

delay_p = delayPre + delayExam + delayPost   (all three stages)
score_p = max(0, 10 − delay_p / 10)           (10 min of waiting = 1 point)
abandoned patients score 0 · no-shows excluded

Promoter ≥ 9 · Passive 7–8 · Detractor ≤ 6

NPS = 100 × (Promoters − Detractors) / everyone who sought care

The MD busy-% penalties come out — patients recommend based on their wait and whether they were seen, and the MD effect already reaches them through delay.
